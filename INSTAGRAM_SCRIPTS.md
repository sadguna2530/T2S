## 🎬 Collection 2: High-Retention Analogy Variations (Day 12 Class)

### **4. Day 12: Master Chef with No Hands (LLM vs Tools)**
> **LLM ki antha telusu, kani okka email kooda enduku pampaledo telusa?**  
> Endukante LLM ante hands leni oka Master Chef lantidi. Brain undi, recipes anni telusu, kani vegetable cut cheyadaniki hands levu!  
> Ah hands e mana "Tools" — Weather API, Gmail, WhatsApp connectivity.  
> Brain ki tools connect chesthene, AI real world lo panulu chestundi.  
> *Idi Day 12 maatrame. Repu Day 13-lo agents build cheyadam nerpukundam.*

---

### **5. Day 12: MCP ante enti? Universal Type-C Cable for AI**
> **Prathi mobile ki okappudu different charger undedi, gurthunda?**  
> Ippudu andaru Type-C adapt chesinatte, AI world lo vachina universal adapter e "MCP" (Model Context Protocol).  
> Inthakumundu Swiggy ki oka API, Uber ki inkoti, BookMyShow ki inkoti custom code rayalsi vachedi.  
> Ippudu MCP unte okka single standard tho ChatGPT or Claude ni mee backend ki plug cheseyochu!  
> Future lo websites lo click chese pani ledu, direct chat lone appointment booked!

---

### **6. Day 12: MCP vs Skills (Kitchen Logic)**
> **AI lo MCP ki Skills ki difference ento okka simple example tho chepthanu.**  
> MCP ante market lo dorike ingredients — vegetables, spices, chicken.  
> Skill ante aa ingredients tho Biryani ela cheyalo cheppe "Recipe"!  
> MCP tools and data ni testhe, Skill step-by-step procedure follow avthundi.  
> Ee rendu unte, AI mee personal assistant la mottham automations run chesthundi.

---

### **7. Day 12: McDonald's AI Chatbot Hack (Prompt Injection Danger)**
> **Oka chatbot ni manipulate chesi company secret recipes kotteyacha?**  
> Yes, deenne "Prompt Injection" antaru. McDonald's valla AI bot ni kontha mandi prompt tricks tho break chesi, millions of tokens waste cheyinchi bot-ne shut down cheyincharu!  
> System prompt entha strong ga unna, AI ki direct payment access or delete permissions eppudu ivvakandi.  
> *Rule okkate: Sensitive actions mundu eppudaina "Human Approval" compulsory ga pettandi.*
